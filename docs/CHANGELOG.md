# VAULT changelog

## V1.1 — UTC payday and shift reports

- Workers submit a same-day UTC shift report (hours + short description)
- Reports are auto-approved and visible to the company owner
- Approved hours pay at 00:00 UTC: hours × hourly rate
- Owners can reject a report; a paid report is clawed back and can put the citizen in debt
- Page gradient now starts at the darkest color
